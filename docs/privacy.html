RecipeBot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

RecipeBot is a private, non-commercial WhatsApp bot used by one family to save cooking recipes from Instagram links into a personal recipe book.

What we collect

  • The WhatsApp phone number that sends a message to the bot, and the text of that message (usually an Instagram link).
  • The recipe extracted from the linked Instagram post (caption, title, ingredients, steps).

How it is used

Messages are used only to extract and save recipes and to reply to the sender. Only pre-approved phone numbers can use the bot; messages from other numbers are ignored. Instagram videos are processed temporarily to read the recipe and are not stored.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data. Data is processed by the service providers that run the bot: Meta (WhatsApp), Google (Gemini AI), Supabase (database) and Render (hosting).

Deletion

To delete your data, contact the owner of the bot through the GitHub repository at github.com/shaharFranses/RecipeBot, and all of your messages and recipes will be deleted.
